Why Join?

Joining the club gives you opportunities to practise programming and meet students with similar interests.

Practical Workshops

Workshops introduce useful technologies through guided exercises. Members create webpages and explore how technology solves problems.

Team Projects

Students collaborate on small projects, explain their decisions, and learn from each other's approaches.

Peer Learning

Members share learning resources and help each other understand difficult topics.

Project Presentations

Students present their work and receive feedback from other members. These sessions help develop communication skills.
