Essential Library Rules
Guidelines for maintaining an optimal study environment for everyone.
  • University ID Required: Always scan your valid JUST ID card at entry gates.
  • Quiet Zones: Maintain absolute silence in designated reading areas on Floors 2 and 3.
  • Food Policy: Covered drinks allowed; open meals prohibited inside study halls.
  • Renewals: Renew books online prior to the due date to avoid overdue fines.
